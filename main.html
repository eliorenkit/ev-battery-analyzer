Simulation only. Temperature is modelled illustratively: cold temperatures reduce available capacity and increase internal resistance, while high temperatures can modestly reduce usable capacity. Real cells vary by chemistry, age, and manufacturer. This software uses an illustrative battery model; readings are not physical sensor measurements and are not calibrated test results. A real battery analyzer needs correctly rated sensors, load hardware, fuse, wiring and thermal protection. Never connect an actual battery to GPIO pins.
